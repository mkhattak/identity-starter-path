# Contributing

Thanks for helping make IAM easier to break into.

- **Ideas or gaps?** Open an issue: what you wish you'd had when you started.
- **Fixes and new material:** send a pull request. Keep it small and focused.
- **Never include customer data.** No configs, names, screenshots or data from any real environment. Use synthetic data.
- **Don't copy vendor docs or training.** Link to them instead.
- **Label AI-assisted content** in your PR description.

By contributing, you agree that your code is licensed under MIT and your written content under CC BY 4.0.
