# AI in the loop

<span class="badge">Experimenting</span>

We're using AI as a study partner and a build assistant, and we'll publish what works (and what doesn't).

## Study companion (planned)
A prompt pack and setup guide that:
- explains IAM and SailPoint concepts at a beginner level
- quizzes you on each curriculum module
- reviews transform, rule and workflow JSON and explains mistakes
- runs incident drills, such as *"a leaver still has directory access, find out why"*

It's built to point you to the official documentation, not to make up product behavior.

## Building with AI
- Generating synthetic identity data and lab scenarios
- Exploring open-source tooling for SailPoint's APIs (we'll check what the community has already built first)

## Our rules for AI
- No customer data goes into any AI tool.
- AI-assisted content is labeled and reviewed by a human.
