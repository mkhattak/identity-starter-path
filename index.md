# Identity Starter Path

<p class="lede">A free, open, hands-on path to becoming a SailPoint identity engineer, built in public by a small team, with help from AI.</p>

Identity and access management (IAM) is one of the hardest skills in security to break into. The tools are enterprise-only, the training is expensive, and there's no realistic data to practice on. So people wait until a job teaches them.

We're trying something different. While we train our own first engineer, we'll publish everything we can: the curriculum, the labs, the practice data and the AI tools we use. That way the next person doesn't have to start from zero.

## What we're building

<div class="cards">
  <div class="card"><h3>📚 Curriculum</h3><p>A 30/60/90-day path from IAM basics to building real SailPoint configurations.</p></div>
  <div class="card"><h3>🧪 Labs</h3><p>Step-by-step exercises with solutions: onboarding sources, joiner-mover-leaver flows, access reviews.</p></div>
  <div class="card"><h3>🧬 Synthetic data</h3><p>A generator for realistic, fake HR and account data, deliberate messes included.</p></div>
  <div class="card"><h3>🤖 AI companion</h3><p>Prompts and tools that explain, quiz and review your work, tied back to the official docs.</p></div>
</div>
