There are three of us: a mentor who does SailPoint work every day, a learner who wants to become an identity engineer, and a sponsor at RedMatter Solutions who thinks more people should get the chance.

On November 9 we met and agreed on a plan: train one engineer, properly and on purpose, and publish everything we can along the way.

## Why in public?

Getting into IAM is hard. The platforms are enterprise products, the courses cost money, and you can't practice on real company data. Most people only learn on the job. We'd like to change that for at least a few people.

## What's next

1. Settle the scope and product track
2. Publish the curriculum outline
3. Build the first lab, plus a generator for fake practice data
4. Try an AI study companion and report back honestly

We'll post here every week. If you're on the same path, follow along, or open an issue and tell us what you wish existed.
